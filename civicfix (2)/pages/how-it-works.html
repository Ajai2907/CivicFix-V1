How CivicFix Works

A seamless, transparent loop connecting citizens with ward field supervisors.

1

Citizen Snaps Photo & Pins Location

When encountering a civic problem (such as a broken streetlight, pothole, or garbage pile), the citizen opens the web app on their smartphone or desktop. The camera or file picker attaches visual proof, and the GPS module automatically acquires exact coordinates (latitude and longitude).

2

Gemini AI Analyzes Severity & Urgency

Our server-side AI model analyzes the submitted photo and text. It determines whether the issue poses an imminent hazard (Critical Severity like live wires or major water pipeline fractures), tags the department, and assigns initial SLA targets.

3

Ticket Generated & Placed in Authority Queue

The citizen receives an instant tracking ticket ID (e.g. CMP-894210). Simultaneously, the municipal command dashboard updates in real time with the new incident on the GIS map.

4

Field Crew Dispatched & Action Logged

Ward supervisors change the ticket status to In Progress and append notes (such as asphalt crew dispatched or valve isolated). When repair work is finished, the ticket is marked as Resolved.

Target Service Level Commitments (SLAs)

Severity Level Example Incident Target Dispatch Target Resolution
Critical Main pipe burst, live wire exposed, road cave-in Within 2 Hours 24 Hours
High Overflowing garbage dump, deep pothole on arterial road Within 6 Hours 48 Hours
Medium Streetlights off, clogged lane drainage Within 24 Hours 72 Hours
Low Faded road marking, cosmetic tree pruning Within 48 Hours 5-7 Days
