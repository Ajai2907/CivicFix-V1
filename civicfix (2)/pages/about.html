About CivicFix

A resilient, crowdsourced civic issue reporting and municipal resolution platform.

The Problem & Vision

Urban civic infrastructure maintenance frequently suffers from fragmented reporting channels, lack of precise geo-spatial tracking, slow triage, and minimal transparency for citizens. CivicFix (SIH25031) connects proactive citizens with municipal public works authorities via an intuitive web application powered by AI visual triage, interactive OpenStreetMap GIS positioning, and real-time resolution dashboards.

Core System Architecture

🤖

Gemini AI Triage

Automatically classifies photo evidence, evaluates severity levels (Critical/High/Medium), and assigns tasks to the proper ward department.

📍

GIS Geo-Tagging

One-touch browser GPS detection and interactive Leaflet map picker ensure pinpoint accuracy for road, water, or electrical crews.

💾

SQLite Persistence

Lightweight, self-contained relational database persists all complaints, ticket statuses, timestamps, and authority logs.

📊

Command Analytics

Chart.js powered breakdown of categories, SLA resolution timelines, and critical hazard hotspots for municipal executives.

Municipal Department Coverage

  • 🛣️ Roads & Highway Infrastructure: Potholes, road subsidence, surface erosion, damaged curbstones.
  • 🗑️ Solid Waste Management: Overflowing community bins, illegal dumps, sanitation hazards.
  • 💧 Water Supply & Sewerage Board: Main pipeline burst, drinking water leak, sewage backflow.
  • 💡 Streetlighting & Electrical: Non-functional fixtures, exposed wiring, dark danger zones.
  • ⚠️ Drainage & Public Safety: Missing manhole covers, storm drain clogging, severe waterlogging.
